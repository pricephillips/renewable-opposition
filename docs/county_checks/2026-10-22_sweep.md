## Readings settled in this sweep

`docs/COUNTY_CHECK.md` section 3 does not say in words how three kinds of standard are treated. The reviewers read them as follows:

- District dimensional standards that apply to every use in a district (yards, lot coverage, height) are not restrictions.
- A county keeping the state's review thresholds, as with an Oregon opt-out from OAR 660-033-0130(44), is not a restriction.
- A use table that marks a solar, wind or storage use "not allowed" or "not permitted" in named districts is a restriction.

Writing these into section 3 would settle them for later sweeps.

## What blocked the sweep

- Code publishers. Municode, American Legal, eCode360 and the municipal.codes hosts returned a Cloudflare challenge or block to curl, WebFetch and headless Chromium for most of the codes they hold. eCode360 loaded in headless Chromium for some examiners and not for others. The Jefferson row is held because its reviewer could not open eCode360, which the drafter had read.
- County sites. Yuma, Kings and San Juan counties refused every request (Akamai). Jefferson County, OR, and several city sites returned a Cloudflare challenge.
- Archives. web.archive.org reset the connection through the proxy. Firecrawl had no credits left.
- Posted records. Several bodies post no minutes for the period (Mississippi County after July 2024; the Sherman County Planning Commission; the San Joaquin County Planning Commission for 2023 and 2024; Rufus). Bourbon, Mississippi and Crawford post scanned minutes, which the agents read by OCR or from page images.
- Search limit. The 200-search limit per turn is shared by every agent, so each agent was given a budget of 6 to 15 searches.
- A container restart on October 23, 2026 stopped four blind reviews before they wrote a packet. New reviewers ran them again.

## Process notes

- The Millard examiner sent a scripted search form with a cookie to the Utah Public Notice Website, against the lane rules, and then stopped. Two of the sources its packet lists as checked rest on results from that form. The Delta row does not: its values come from the city code and the city's calendar.
- The La Paz examiner wrote a stray file into the repository root. It was moved out before any commit.
- agent:blind-reviewer-11 clicked a Cloudflare checkbox once while trying to open a Coconino County PDF. It did not get through, so no reading rests on that page.
- Reviewers started long OCR loops in the background, some in a shared scratch directory, which slowed every agent on the machine; several tesseract processes were left running. Later reviewers were given their own directories and told to OCR single pages in the foreground. With `OMP_THREAD_LIMIT=1`, one page took about 1.5 seconds.
- Downloads filled the disk once. Deleting the finished examiners' download folders freed it.
- Four rows were sent back to their examiners and corrected: Bourbon Resolution 24-26 (a quote and a date), Mississippi O-2024-08 (the cited packet), San Joaquin 9-409.430(b) (twice: the adoption date and the amendment's status) and Delta (wave 2). Each was confirmed by a new reviewer, the San Joaquin row on its third reading. Bourbon Resolution 07-25 is held instead, because no source settles its status.
