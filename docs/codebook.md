# Codebook

Generated by `scripts/codebook.py` from the constants the pipeline runs on; do not edit.
Rebuilt on every data build. `README.md` explains the rules in prose; this file is what the
code does. A table that disagrees with the README means the README is out of date.

## Restriction severity

`build_sabin_seeds.restriction_severity` runs on the mechanisms a row names, its technology, status
and text. Each line below is the rule's own answer for one probe; `severity_basis` is the
text the row carries. A pending instrument is capped at 2.

| Probe | Technology | Status | Score | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ban or prohibition | solar | active | 4 | ban/prohibition |
| Ban or moratorium, source does not say which, in force | solar | active | 4 | ban or moratorium (the source does not say which) |
| Moratorium in force | solar | active | 4 | in-force moratorium |
| Moratorium, extended | wind | extended | 4 | in-force moratorium |
| Moratorium, pending | solar | pending | 2 | restricting mechanism (default) |
| Wind setback of half a mile | wind | active | 3 | wind setback 2640 ft |
| Wind setback of 5x turbine height | wind | active | 3 | wind setback 5x height |
| Wind setback of 1,500 feet | wind | active | 2 | restricting mechanism (default) |
| Wind height limit | wind | active | 3 | wind height limit |
| Wind noise limit of 35 dBA | wind | active | 3 | wind noise limit 35 dBA |
| Wind noise limit of 45 dBA | wind | active | 2 | restricting mechanism (default) |
| Solar setback of 1,000 feet | solar | active | 3 | solar setback 1000 ft |
| Solar setback of 500 feet | solar | active | 2 | restricting mechanism (default) |
| Battery storage setback of 1,000 feet | battery_storage | active | 3 | battery_storage setback 1000 ft |
| Battery storage spacing of 1,500 feet between sites | battery_storage | active | 2 | restricting mechanism (default) |
| Size cap | solar | active | 2 | restricting mechanism (default) |
| Zoning restriction | solar | active | 2 | restricting mechanism (default) |
| Any restricting mechanism, pending | solar | pending | 2 | solar setback 1000 ft; capped at 2 (pending) |

The mechanism that drives the score names `restriction_type` (`driving_type`). Mechanism
spellings accepted in a review-queue row and the type each maps to:

| Type | Spellings |
|---|---|
| `ban` | `ban/prohibition`, `ban`, `primary use prohibition` |
| `ban_or_moratorium` | `ban or moratorium` |
| `height_limit` | `height_limit`, `height limit`, `height restriction`, `height limit with resident permission` |
| `moratorium` | `moratorium`, `pause on new projects`, `extension` |
| `noise_limit` | `noise_limit`, `noise limit` |
| `other` | `refusal to sign agreements`, `extended_producer_responsibility` |
| `setback` | `setback`, `setback rules`, `buffer zone` |
| `siting_standard` | `shadow_flicker_limit`, `visual_impact_rule` |
| `size_cap` | `cap on acreage`, `cap on project size`, `cap on area`, `area limit`, `cap on capacity`, `cap on project area` |
| `zoning_restriction` | `zoning_restriction`, `zoning restriction`, `overlay_zone`, `ag_land_exclusion`, `zoning regulation update`, `land preservation program` |

Non-restricting mechanisms (never scored): `preemption`.
Statuses the source may give and the status published: `in_force` -> `active`, `extended` -> `extended`, `pending` -> `pending`, `unknown` -> `unknown`, `(blank)` -> `unknown`.
Statuses not published (the instrument is not in force): `cancelled`, `expired`, `lifted`; a
review-queue restriction may carry `active`, `extended`, `pending`, `lifted`, `expired`, of which
`expired`, `lifted` stay in the queue.

## Contested projects: outcome and severity

A source status maps to an outcome (`build_sabin_seeds.OUTCOME`); a status alone is never
`*_confirmed`. A linked court case decided the same way confirms it (`CONFIRMING_RULING`), as does a
reviewer's resolution whose evidence was opened or archived (`scripts/resolutions.py`).

| Source status | Outcome |
|---|---|
| `cancelled` | `blocked_unverified` |
| `rejected` | `blocked_unverified` |
| `approved` | `advanced_unverified` |
| `approved_after_opposition` | `advanced_unverified` |
| `operational` | `advanced_unverified` |
| `pending` | `pending` |
| `proposed` | `pending` |

| Unverified outcome | Confirming case status | Confirmed outcome |
|---|---|---|
| `blocked_unverified` | `ruled_for_opposition` | `blocked_confirmed` |
| `advanced_unverified` | `ruled_for_developer` | `advanced_confirmed` |

`build_sabin_seeds.project_severity(outcome, litigated)`:

| Outcome | Not litigated | Litigated |
|---|---|---|
| `advanced_confirmed` | 1 | 3 |
| `advanced_unverified` | 1 | 3 |
| `blocked_confirmed` | 4 | 4 |
| `blocked_unverified` | 4 | 4 |
| `needs_review` | 2 | 3 |
| `pending` | 2 | 3 |
| `restricted_conditional` | 2 | 3 |

Finality evidence that satisfies the QC gate: a value starting with `court_ruling`, `resolution`. `lead: <url>` (a snippet resolution) and `outcome_label_only` do not.

## Derived fields (`scripts/classify.py`)

- `evidence_level`, best first: `primary_source`, `confirmed`, `court_record`, `compiled_record`, `compiled_flagged`, `report_citation`.
- `verification`: `verified`, `located`, `unverified`. A restriction is verified only against the instrument or the minutes that adopted it; opposition activity may rest on a news article that was read.
- `scope` (restrictions): `renewables_only`, `multi_sector_data_centers`, `data_center_only`. `data_center_only` is quarantined.
- `currency` (restrictions): `in_force`, `lapsed_unconfirmed`, `pending`, `unknown`. In force means status `active`, `extended` with no end date passed; lapsed_unconfirmed means the end date passed and no reviewer confirmed the instrument on or after it.
- `status_as_of` (restrictions): the latest of an 'as of YYYY-MM-DD' date in the text, the enactment date, or a reviewer's primary-source check.
- `state_limits_local` (restrictions below state level): `yes`, `no`, `unknown`, read off the state's verified `state_policies` rows for the technology: yes when a local_preemption row or a size threshold covers it or the state board decides outright, no when local government decides with no such rule, unknown when no verified row covers the technology (or only a hybrid row with no threshold). Carried with `state_siting_authority`, `state_threshold_mw`, `state_preemption_ids`. Context only; never says an instrument is void.
- `source_kind` of a queue row: `instrument`, `minutes`, `official_copy`, `court_record`, `news`, `tracker`, `other`; only `instrument`, `minutes`, `official_copy` can verify a restriction; `instrument`, `minutes`, `official_copy`, `court_record`, `news` can verify a project outcome or event.
- How evidence was seen (`access`): `opened`, `archived`, `snippet`; only `archived`, `opened` upgrade a record. Primary-source verdicts: `confirmed`, `contradicts`.

## QC checks (`scripts/qc_gate.py`)

A row with any CRITICAL or HIGH issue is quarantined in `data/processed/quarantine.json`; lower severities are reported in `data/processed/qc_report.md`.

| Code | Severity | Meaning | What releases a held row |
|---|---|---|---|
| `COORD_OUTSIDE_STATE` | HIGH | coordinates not inside the state the row names | correct the coordinates or the state in the seed (or the source's row) so they agree |
| `SOURCE_URL_INVALID` | HIGH | source_url, case_source_url or primary_source_url is not http(s) | replace the value with the document's http(s) address |
| `STATUS_VOCAB` | HIGH | status, outcome, case_status or a derived field outside the entity's vocabulary | map the source's value onto the vocabulary in the importer (build_sabin_seeds, fetch_moratorium_nation) or the queue row |
| `DATE_IN_FUTURE` | HIGH | an enactment or filing date after the build date | check the date against the instrument or docket; a proposal date belongs in date_text, not date_enacted_iso |
| `SEVERITY_STATUS` | MEDIUM | a pending instrument scored above 2 | nothing to do: reported only; the score is capped at 2 while pending |
| `CONFIRMED_NO_EVIDENCE` | HIGH | a *_confirmed outcome with no independent finality evidence | add a data/review/outcome_resolutions.csv row with the document that shows the outcome (access opened or archived), or set the outcome back to *_unverified |
| `PRIMARY_SOURCE_CONTRADICTS` | MEDIUM or HIGH | a reviewer found the primary source disagrees with the record; MEDIUM when that source was seen only as search-index text (a lead to read), HIGH when it was opened or archived | correct the record to what the primary source says (a queue row, or a note to the upstream source), then set the restriction_sources.csv verdict to confirmed |
| `SCOPE_DATA_CENTER_ONLY` | HIGH | a restriction whose text names data centers and no renewable technology | if the instrument does cover a renewable technology, say so in the record's text or sectors; if not, it belongs to data-center-map and stays out |
| `DATE_UNPARSEABLE` | LOW | an ISO date column that is not YYYY[-MM[-DD]] | nothing to do: reported only; rewrite the date as YYYY, YYYY-MM or YYYY-MM-DD when touching the row |
| `LINK_MISSING` | MEDIUM | a case whose source_record_id names no published project or restriction | set the case's source_record_id to the published record it concerns, or restore that record (it may be quarantined or held); the case stays published meanwhile |
| `END_DATE_PASSED` | MEDIUM | an active or extended restriction whose own end date has passed with no reviewer confirmation since; the source's status may be out of date | nothing to do here: reported only; the restriction worklist lists it first, and a restriction_sources.csv row checked on or after the end date restores it to in_force |

## Counting (`scripts/headline_metrics.py`)

- One instrument counts once, whatever technologies it covers; its severity is the highest of its rows and its verification the weakest.
- Severe means a severity score in `3`, `4`.
- Restrictions are split by scope; the headline figure is renewables_only and multi_sector_data_centers is reported beside it, never folded in.
- Source families: `Sabin 2026`, `Sabin 2025 (not in 2026 edition)`, `NREL`, `Moratorium Nation`, `review queue`, `other`.
- Decided projects: an outcome in `blocked_confirmed`, `blocked_unverified`, `advanced_confirmed`, `advanced_unverified`, `restricted_conditional`, with events younger than 90 days left out (the right-censoring guard). The blocked share is reported for all decided projects and for confirmed outcomes alone, and is conditional on opposition being tracked at all.
- `county_severe_restriction_ids`: severe (score 3 or more) renewables-only instruments for the same technology in any county the project touches; counted as projects sharing a county with one.

## Worklists (`scripts/verification_worklist.py`)

Outcome tiers, in the order checked, and what would confirm each:

| Tier | What would confirm it |
|---|---|
| `blocked_unverified` | Withdrawal or cancellation notice, permit or zoning denial, or interconnection queue withdrawal |
| `advanced_unverified` | Permit or zoning approval, construction start, or commercial operation (EIA-860, utility or developer announcement) |
| `needs_review` | The project's current status from county records or recent coverage |
| `pending` | Any decision since the event date: approval, denial or withdrawal |

Restriction tiers, weakest evidence first: `compiled_flagged`, `compiled_record`, `report_citation`. An instrument whose currency is `lapsed_unconfirmed` is listed first whatever its tier.

- A pending restriction is stale after 120 days (from `status_as_of`).
- A pending project is overdue after 365 days (from the latest date in `event_date_text`, at the end of its precision).
- `decision_language` lists words in a pending row's own text that state a completed decision (pattern `\b(approved|adopted|enacted|denied|rejected|withdrawn|withdrew|cancell?ed|abandoned|dismissed|granted|upheld|overturned|unanimously (approved|adopted|voted|rejected|denied)|voted (down|to approve|to deny|to reject))\b`) unless any word says the step was not final (pattern `\b(initial|first reading|preliminary|tentative(ly)?|consider(s|ing)?|weigh(s|ing)?|to vote|will vote|set to|plans? to|propos(e|es|ed|al)|draft(s|ing)?|move[sd]? toward|towards?|hearing|could|may|seeks?|push(es)? for|calls? for|urge[sd]?|asks?|appeal(s|ed|ing)?|pending|lawsuit|sue[sd]?|suit|challeng(e|es|ed|ing)|petition(s|ed)?|intervene[sd]?|remand(s|ed)?)\b`).

## Negative checks (`scripts/negative_checks.py`)

- Scope: `restrictions`, `projects`, `both`; result: `none_found`; stale after 365 days.
- Independence classes of `sources_checked`: `primary_law`, `proceeding`, `secondary`. A grade counts classes, never sources.

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `A` | two or more independence classes checked, not stale |
| `B` | one class checked and not stale, or two or more classes but stale |
| `C` | one class checked, stale |
| `U` | no source classed |

## Operations summary (`scripts/operations_summary.py`)

`data/processed/operations_summary.json` and `.md` count the rows other modules wrote: the headline, the quarantine by blocking code, each worklist's size and flags, the promotion trail by action, the review queue by status, the snapshot and headline-history rows, and the share of sources with an archive copy. Nothing in it is computed anywhere else first.

## Promotion trail (`scripts/promote_reviewed.py`)

`data/review/promotion_trail.csv` records a decision only when it is new or has changed
(`scripts/promotion_trail.py`). Columns: `decided_on`, `file`, `key`, `entity_type`, `state`, `place`, `action`, `reason`. Actions: `promoted`, `held`
(with what the row lacks), `awaiting_review`, `rejected`, `error`. Review statuses that never promote: `promoted`, `rejected`.
