Written by `scripts/nrel_sample.py report`. 60 NREL-derived restriction instruments drawn with `random.Random(20261009)` from the sorted list of every NREL restriction (nrel_solar_ordinances_2025=8bceb085ddb76117176c326aa42b35fe0eca983a2ebc1760fca57ce32320dca7;nrel_wind_ordinances_2025=1178c79bff4eb88ed423639ccea0af3b1120d4b63c32ae5138bd2fdcacf9268f).
