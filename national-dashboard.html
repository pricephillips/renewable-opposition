Counts are of instruments, projects and cases: one ordinance counts once however many technologies it covers. Restrictions that also cover data centers are counted beside the renewables figure, never inside it. A restriction is verified only against the instrument itself or the minutes that adopted it; a news article or a compiled tracker locates it but does not verify it. A county nobody has examined is shown as not examined, never as zero.

Sources: Sabin Center for Climate Change Law, Opposition to Renewable Energy Facilities in the United States (September 2026 edition); NREL U.S. Wind and Solar Siting Regulation and Zoning Ordinances (2025), CC BY 4.0; Moratorium Nation (mjbommar/moratorium-data-2026), CC BY 4.0; Data Center Tracker via pricephillips/data-center-map, CC BY 4.0.
